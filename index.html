Hornpoke Industries Advanced Research Open-Source

Exploring ambitious ideas in computer science through human-AI collaboration.

Hornpoke Industries develops ideas through original, unedited ChatGPT sessions. These sessions document the brainstorming, reasoning, hypothesis formation, prototype development, and experimental planning as they actually occur.

Our Open-Source Process

01 — Original Sessions

We publish the original ChatGPT conversations, unadulterated, as part of the research record.

02 — Hypothesis & Experiment

Ideas become hypotheses, prototypes, and experiments that can be examined, challenged, reproduced, and extended.

03 — Whitepaper

When sufficient material has been developed, the work may be consolidated into a formal whitepaper.

04 — Community

Researchers, developers, and curious minds are invited to examine the work and contribute.

05 — Funding

The research is open and unfunded. If the community wants a project taken further, voluntary donations can provide development funding.

06 — Professional Development

Funded projects may engage professional contractors to advance the technology while maintaining its open-source character.

07 — Daily Session Notes

Developers collaborate with ChatGPT before and after each workday. The resulting session notes document the work and provide an ongoing public development record.

08 — Where the Project Stops

A project may stop when the development team moves on, available funding is exhausted, or the experiment or prototype reaches a technical barrier that cannot presently be overcome.

A stopped project remains part of the open-source research record. Future researchers may resume it, learn from it, or take it in a different direction.

The Principle

Research openly. Test rigorously. Document the process. Build what people want to see built.
